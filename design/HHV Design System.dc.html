Venue template · 12 seconds Short-form venue spots perform best at roughly 7–15 seconds with the hook in the first second, so this runs five beats in 12s and loops cleanly. 0–1.8s Hook: the strongest deal as the headline ($28 pizza + a bottle of wine). Lead with price, not the venue name. 1.8–4.2s Venue: listing photo with a slow push-in, lower-third plate (area, name, address) and the menu/vibe/price chips. 4.2–6.8s Hero deal: gold halftone block, number counts to 28, pizza options underneath. 6.8–9.2s $5 menu: the three $5 drinks only, staggered in. 9.2–12s When + end: week strip with Mon–Fri lit, 4–6pm at the bar, then the lockup and "Full menu · link in bio". Left out: the six food items, phone number and the long editorial blurb. They're too much to read at this length; the link in bio covers them. Swap the hero deal per venue; everything else maps to listing fields.

Four beats, one idea each, teal ground throughout. Entrances use the brand pop ease (cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1)); exits are 200ms fades. Numbers count up, bars grow from the left, and the end card replays the logo animation. Keep each beat readable as a still, since Instagram picks a cover frame from it.

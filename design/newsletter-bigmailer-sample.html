Lexie's Bistro Happy Hour
👉 Italian Happy Hour
⏰ Mon – Fri 4 p.m. to 6 p.m.
📍 Henderson (Raiders Way)
Lexie’s is everything a neighborhood bistro should be - warm, personal, and completely unbothered by what’s happening on the Strip. The name comes from the owners’ granddaughter Lexie, who also decorated the room with owls because she loves them. That detail tells you everything you need to know about the kind of place this is. The happy hour menu skews Italian but has something for everyone from chicken and eggplant parm to chicken wings and salmon cakes all between $10 and $15. The $28 bottle and pizza deal is the kind of offer that makes you want to call someone and say “meet me here at 4.” No visitors. No tourists. Just locals, good food, owls and Raiders Football. My kind of happy hour!
Atomic Golf Happy Hour
👉 AYCD Golf Happy Hour
⏰ Mon - Fri 12 p.m. to 4 p.m. (Fri - Sat 9 p.m - midnight)
📍 North Strip (The Strat)
$49 per person gets you a reserved bay, unlimited golf, cocktails, and food for two hours. This is not a drink discount, it's an all-inclusive package and it’s a good one regardless of your handicap. I’ve covered a lot of happy hours in this city and the Atomic Golf package is genuinely in a category of its own. You're not getting a discounted mojito and a bucket of balls. You're getting unlimited everything and if you want to stay longer, add an extra hour for $25 per person. The Late Night Unlimited package on Friday and Saturday from 9 PM to midnight runs $59 per person and is equally absurd in the best way. This one books fast so plan ahead. Fore!

Pasta Cucina Happy Hour
👉 Italian Happy Hour
⏰ Daily 8 p.m. to Close
📍 Henderson (Sunset Station Resort)
The only happy hour in Las Vegas with Cacio e Pepe on the menu. Eight items, $8 each, starting at 8 PM. They call it 8 for $8 at 8 and that's the math. I’ve been tracking happy hours in this city for almost as long as my love for Cacio e Pepe started and nobody else in Las Vegas is putting it on the happy hour menu for $8. The kitchen at Pasta Cucina does an excellent job with authentic Italian-American dishes and the simplicity of this program is the whole point. Eight items. Eight dollars each. Every night from 8 PM to close at the bar. Scampi, Cacio e Pepe, and a cocktail or two. That’s a late night Italian happy hour that feels like a reward at the end of a long day. And if you want to try Pasta Cucina’s signature bites alongside about 15 other restaurants in one place, save the date for National Happy Hour Day on November 12 at Sunset Station. Subscribers to the happy hour club get priority ticket access. Stay tuned.

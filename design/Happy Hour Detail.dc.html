About this happy hour
Spy Ninjas HQ Happy Hour A $10 pizza slice and draft beer combo inside a neon-lit retro arcade at an indoor adventure park in Summerlin. The Spy Ninjas HQ happy hour at the Retro Bar + Arcade is genuinely one of the more unexpected happy hours in Las Vegas, and it works precisely because it’s not trying to be a traditional bar. The happy hour runs weekdays 4–8 PM, with $4 draft beers (Coors Light, Dos Equis), $6 house wines, and $7 well drinks. The food menu is where it gets interesting. Spy Ninjas HQ Happy Hour Menu with Prices
Slice & a Beer — $10 — one slice of any menu pizza plus a happy hour draft. The best value on the menu, full stop. Wings (6pc) — $11 — grilled, with nine sauce or rub options including Hot Honey and Hotter Than Hades. Quesadilla — $11.50 — smoked pork carnitas, beef barbacoa, carne asada, or grilled chicken, with caramelized onions, grilled corn, chipotle crema, and cotija. Chips, Salsa & Guacamole — $5 — housemade tortilla chips, fresh guac, fire ninja red or charred tomatillo salsa.
The Retro Bar is built for adults who come with kids or adults who just want to play Tron and Ms. Pac-Man with a $7 well drink in hand. The four-hour happy hour window (4–8 PM) is longer than most in Las Vegas, and the Summerlin location at 7980 W. Sahara gives the west side a genuine family-friendly happy hour option that doesn’t require a casino floor to get there. If you haven’t brought the kids here yet, the Slice & a Beer deal is reason enough to go tonight.
